About Us

Building strong foundations for tomorrow's engineers.

The Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Motihari College of Engineering provides a learning environment that combines strong fundamentals, practical laboratory experience, innovation and professional development.

Students are encouraged to participate in technical activities, projects, workshops, competitions, industrial exposure and co-curricular clubs.

Electrical EngineeringElectronicsPower SystemsControlEmbedded Systems
VISION

To develop technically competent, innovative and socially responsible electrical and electronics engineers.

MISSION

Provide quality education, practical skills, research exposure and opportunities for innovation and professional growth.

Laboratories

Hands-on learning through modern laboratories.

01

Electrical Machines Lab

Study transformers, DC machines, induction motors and electrical machine characteristics.

02

Power Systems Lab

Explore transmission, distribution, protection and power-system analysis.

03

Power Electronics Lab

Practical study of converters, inverters, controlled rectifiers and drives.

04

Control Systems Lab

Learn modelling, feedback systems, stability and controller implementation.

05

Measurements Lab

Understand electrical measurement techniques, instruments and transducers.

06

Microprocessor & Embedded Lab

Work with 8085, 8051, microcontrollers and embedded applications.

07

DSP / Electronics Lab

Explore digital signal processing, filters and electronics experiments.

08

Simulation Lab

Use engineering software to model and simulate electrical systems.
